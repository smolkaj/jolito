Update Jolito safely

If cloud sync asks you to update, reports a deck schema mismatch, or says “permission denied for table decks,” your app needs the latest sync support. Your local deck can stay on this device while you update.

Keep your work first

Return to the app and finish or save any card you are editing. Complete your current practice answer before closing it. An unfinished answer or unsaved draft will not survive closing the app.

Export a JSON deck backup from Manage deck → Backup & Import → Export backup (JSON). If a save or export fails, keep the app open and copy unfinished text somewhere safe before continuing.

Keep the app installed and keep its site data. Clearing browser storage or uninstalling can erase cards that have not synced.

Browser or home-screen app

Connect to the internet. Open Jolito once so the browser can download the update, then close every Jolito tab and window, including the home-screen app. Close this guide too. Reopen Jolito in the same browser or home-screen app and choose Cloud sync → Sync now.

A reload alone may keep the old version while another Jolito tab is open. If the update was still downloading, repeat the close-and-reopen step once it has finished. If sync says it is being updated, keep your local deck and retry shortly.

Native iPhone or iPad app

Install the latest Jolito update from the same place you installed the app, such as TestFlight. Reopening a native app does not download new app code. Keep the existing installation and its data; then open it and sync again.

If sync still fails

Keep your backup and local app data. You can continue practicing locally. Use Cloud sync → Feedback to report the message, or email a@joli.to. Do not delete your account to repair sync.
