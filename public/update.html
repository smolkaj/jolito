Update Jolito safely

Your cards stay safe on this device while you update.

1. Protect your cards

  • Finish or save any open card draft or practice answer.
  • Export a backup from Manage deck → Backup & Import → Export backup (JSON).
  • Do not clear browser data or uninstall the app. Unsynced cards exist only on this device.

2. Apply the update

Web or home screen:

  1. Close all Jolito tabs and windows, including this guide.
  2. Reopen Jolito and tap Cloud sync → Sync now.

iPhone or iPad:

Update Jolito from TestFlight or the App Store, reopen, and sync.

Need help?

You can keep practicing offline. Never delete your account to fix sync. Report issues via Cloud sync → Feedback or email a@joli.to.
