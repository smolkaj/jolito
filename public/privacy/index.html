Jolito

Privacy Policy

Local-first by design. Zero tracking, zero ads, zero surveillance.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

1. Architecture: Local-First by Default

Jolito is built local-first. Your flashcards, learning schedules, review statistics, and speech audio cache remain stored exclusively on your device within your browser's local database (IndexedDB and Web Storage). If you choose to use Jolito offline or without an account, zero personal data ever leaves your device.

2. Optional Cloud Synchronization

If you opt in to multi-device sync, we ask for your email address solely to transmit a passwordless sign-in magic link. Upon authentication:

  • Your flashcard decks and review timestamps are replicated to an encrypted Supabase database.
  • Database access is strictly governed by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring only your authenticated account can view or update your cards.
  • We never use your email address for marketing campaigns or newsletters, and we never sell your email to third parties.

3. In-App Account and Data Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and global privacy principles:

  • You can permanently delete your cloud account and all cloud-synced flashcards at any time directly inside the Jolito app (under Cloud sync → Delete cloud account & data).
  • Deletion is immediate and permanent across our cloud replication storage.
  • Local flashcards stored in your device storage remain entirely under your personal control.

4. Pronunciation Audio & Neural Speech

Jolito provides authentic Mexican Spanish pronunciation audio via device speech synthesis and low-latency edge audio streaming. Jolito does not record or access your device microphone, does not capture voice recordings, and does not store voice biometric templates.

5. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Zero Advertising

Jolito operates under strict $0.00 operating principles. We do not incorporate Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, advertising SDKs, or third-party behavioral telemetry. We do not track you across apps or websites.

6. Open Source Transparency & Contact

Jolito is open-source under the Apache-2.0 License. The source code is publicly inspectable on GitHub at github.com/smolkaj/jolito.

If you have questions, privacy requests, or feedback, please contact Steffen Smolka at steffen.smolka@gmail.com or submit in-app feedback.
