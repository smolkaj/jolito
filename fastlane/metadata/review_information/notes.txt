2. APP PURPOSE & TARGET AUDIENCE
================================================================================
Jolito is a dedicated, distraction-free spaced repetition flashcard app engineered specifically for learners of Mexican Spanish. While standard language apps often teach Peninsular (European) Spanish or formal textbook idioms, Jolito focuses on authentic Mexican Spanish vocabulary, colloquial expressions, and grammar nuances used daily in Mexico. It provides immediate value to travelers, expatriates, and serious language students by combining natural Mexican Spanish pronunciation audio with an evidence-based spaced repetition memory schedule.

================================================================================
3. SETUP & ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================
Jolito is designed with a local-first architecture:
- No account or login credentials are required to use the app.
- All core features—including practicing cards, listening to Mexican Spanish audio, creating new cards, and viewing deck statistics—are immediately accessible upon cold launch without an account.
- Optional Cloud Sync: Users who wish to sync their flashcards across devices may tap the "Account" / "Sync" icon in the navigation bar to sign in via "Sign in with Apple" or via email one-time passcode (OTP).
- For review testing of cloud sync, reviewers may use "Sign in with Apple" directly or enter any valid test email address to receive an OTP code.

================================================================================
4. EXTERNAL SERVICES & PLATFORMS
================================================================================
Jolito uses minimal external infrastructure to preserve user privacy and operating simplicity:
- Supabase (PostgreSQL & Auth): Powers optional cloud sync, authentication (Sign in with Apple and Email OTP), and immediate account/data deletion.
- Bundled Mexican Spanish Audio / Cloudflare CDN: Pre-rendered neural audio files stored locally and cached via Cloudflare CDN for low latency.
- No third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics, or data brokers are integrated into the application.

================================================================================

The app functions identically across all App Store territories worldwide without regional differences, geofencing, or localized feature locks. While the curriculum focuses specifically on the Mexican Spanish dialect (es-MX), learners anywhere in the world have identical access to all features.
