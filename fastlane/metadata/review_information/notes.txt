1. RECORDING AND FUNCTIONALITY
Attached: native-walkthrough.mp4. Mirror:

This is the bundled iOS app captured in Apple's iPhone Air Simulator on iOS 27, with native status bar, touch gestures and original app audio.
The recording starts with app launch and demonstrates personal cards, real email-code sign-in, signed-in study, automatic pronunciation and account deletion using a separate disposable account. The reviewer account remains available.
Cards are private study material: no public feed, public sharing or user-to-user interaction. Content reporting/blocking does not apply. The app costs US$2.99 upfront (local equivalents set by Apple); no subscriptions, IAP or feature paywalls.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
For Mexican Spanish learners, travelers and residents who want to remember useful phrases and verb forms. Learners create personal flashcards, hear pronunciation and practice recall with spaced repetition.

3. SETUP AND REVIEWER ACCESS
The starter demo works without an account. Personal card creation, imports and synchronization require free sign-in and an initial internet connection. Subsequent study works offline; pronunciation uses installed device voices. There is one learner account type; no sample files required.
Use the username in App Review's sign-in fields as the email in Jolito, then tap Send sign-in link. To read the code, open https://mail.tm/en/ in a browser, choose Account > Log in, and use BOTH credentials from App Review's sign-in fields. The password is for this dedicated email inbox, not a Jolito password. Open the latest Jolito email and enter its six-digit code in Jolito, then tap Sign in. Request a fresh code for each login. This inbox and account are dedicated to review; no developer intervention is needed.
Create: enter Spanish and English, optionally context/both directions, then Save card. Practice: recall, swipe up or tap Reveal answer, listen, then swipe right for Good or tap a rating. Audio plays automatically; speaker buttons replay it. Deck: browse/search/edit cards and add starter packs. Grammar: Home > Practice menu > Grammar > Start practice. Delete: Account/Sync > Delete cloud account & data, read the warning, type DELETE and confirm.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase: authentication, database and sync. Apple: Sign in with Apple. Resend: authentication/transactional email. Cloudflare: web/API hosting and Workers AI for optional example/mnemonic suggestions. iOS pronunciation: device speech voices (the web version uses a separate cloud speech service). Bundled dictionary: Wiktionary/Kaikki. Mail.tm provides only the dedicated reviewer inbox, not app functionality. No advertising or third-party tracking SDKs; first-party aggregate telemetry is described in the privacy policy.

5. REGIONS
No intentional regional feature or curriculum variation. Content focuses on Mexican Spanish (es-MX). Apple manages store availability/prices; installed voices and online-service availability may vary.
