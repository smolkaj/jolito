2. APP PURPOSE & TARGET AUDIENCE
Jolito is a distraction-free spaced repetition flashcard app engineered specifically for Mexican Spanish learners (travelers, expatriates, and students). It focuses on authentic Mexican vocabulary, slang, and grammar with native pronunciation audio and evidence-based SRS scheduling.

3. SETUP & ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
- Local-first architecture: No account or credentials required to use the app.
- All core features (practice, audio, custom cards, deck manager) are immediately available upon launch.
- Optional Cloud Sync: Users can tap "Account" / "Sync" to sign in via "Sign in with Apple" or email OTP.
- For review testing, reviewers can use "Sign in with Apple" directly or enter any valid test email for an OTP.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES & PLATFORMS
- Supabase (PostgreSQL & Auth): Optional cloud sync, authentication (Apple Sign-In & Email OTP), and immediate account/data deletion.
- Bundled Mexican Spanish Audio / Cloudflare CDN: Pre-rendered neural audio files cached via Cloudflare CDN.
- No third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or analytics brokers.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Functions identically worldwide with zero regional differences, geofencing, or localized feature locks. The curriculum focuses on Mexican Spanish (es-MX).

6. REGULATORY & THIRD-PARTY RIGHTS
Jolito does not operate in a regulated industry. All vocabulary, artwork (mascot Jolito), audio, and code are original or permissively licensed open-source. Contains no protected third-party IP.
