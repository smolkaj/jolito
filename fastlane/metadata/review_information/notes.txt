Updated information for Jolito 1.0, addressing Guideline 2.1:

1. RECORDING AND FUNCTIONALITY
Attached: native-walkthrough.mp4. Mirror:
https://github.com/smolkaj/jolito/raw/refs/heads/main/docs/media/native-walkthrough.mp4
This is the bundled iOS app captured in Apple's iPhone Air Simulator on iOS 27, with native status bar, touch gestures and original app audio.
The recording starts with app launch and demonstrates personal cards, real email-code sign-in, signed-in study, automatic pronunciation and account deletion using a separate disposable account. The reviewer account remains available.
Cards are private study material: no public feed, public sharing or user-to-user interaction. Content reporting/blocking does not apply. The app costs US$2.99 upfront (local equivalents set by Apple); no subscriptions, IAP or feature paywalls.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
For Mexican Spanish learners, travelers and residents who want to remember useful phrases and verb forms. Learners create personal flashcards, hear pronunciation and practice recall with spaced repetition.

3. SETUP AND REVIEWER ACCESS
The starter demo works without an account. Personal card creation, imports and synchronization require free sign-in and an initial internet connection. Subsequent study works offline; pronunciation uses installed device voices. There is one learner account type; no sample files required.
Reviewers can sign in directly inside the app using the demo account credentials provided in App Store Connect: open Account/Sync, enter the username from App Review's sign-in fields into the "Email address" field, tap "Sign in with password", enter the password, and tap "Sign in". (Alternatively, you may enter the username under "Email address", tap "Send sign-in link", and enter the demo password in the code field). No external email client or web browser is required.
Create: enter Spanish and English, optionally context/both directions, then Save card. Cards: recall, swipe up or tap Reveal answer, listen, then swipe right for Good or tap a rating. Audio plays automatically; speaker buttons replay it. Deck: browse/search/edit cards and add starter packs. Grammar: Grammar tab > Start practice. Delete: Account/Sync > Delete cloud account & data, read the warning, type DELETE and confirm.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase: authentication, database and sync. Resend: transactional email for learners using email sign-in links. Cloudflare: web/API hosting and Workers AI for optional example/mnemonic suggestions. iOS pronunciation: device speech voices (the web version uses a separate cloud speech service). Bundled dictionary: Wiktionary/Kaikki. No advertising or third-party tracking SDKs; first-party aggregate telemetry is described in the privacy policy.

5. REGIONS
No intentional regional feature or curriculum variation. Content focuses on Mexican Spanish (es-MX). Apple manages store availability/prices; installed voices and online-service availability may vary.
