Make the words you meet stick.

Jolito helps you learn Mexican Spanish with spoken flashcards, active recall, and spaced repetition. Build a personal collection of the words and phrases you want to remember, then practice at your own rhythm.

• Create linked Spanish and English flashcards, with an offline dictionary and verb suggestions.
• Practice by typing an answer, comparing it, and choosing when to review again.
• Hear pronunciation using your device's speech voices. Mexican Spanish voice quality and availability depend on the voices installed on your device.
• Bring your existing Anki decks and keep your learning history.
• Study offline after signing in, then sync your cards and progress when connected.
• Export your deck and delete your cloud account from within the app.

Try the starter demo without an account. Creating a personal deck, importing cards, and saving progress require free email sign-in and an initial internet connection. Subsequent study works offline; offline speech requires installed device voices.
